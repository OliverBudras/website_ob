01 / About

Welcome!

I am a researcher at Leibniz University Hannover working at the intersection of financial economics and computational methods.

My research uses textual data, machine learning, and empirical asset-pricing methods to investigate how information relates to financial markets. Among others, my work has examined uncertainty in corporate filings, the pricing implications of localized risk factors, the informational content of media reporting for financial markets, and the relation of environmental information to the financial performance of companies.

Alongside financial economics, I am involved in research on model transfer, which focuses on the use of computational methods to analyze the transfer of models across scientific disciplines.

02 / Research

Research interests

01

Asset Pricing & Financial Markets

Empirical asset pricing, risk factors, investor responses to information, and the role of uncertainty in financial markets.

02

Text as Data, NLP & Machine Learning

Methods for extracting economically meaningful signals from corporate disclosures and news, including language-model-based approaches.

03

Sustainable Finance

Text-based measures of environmental, social, and governance information and their implications for valuation and capital markets.

04

Model Transfer & Science Mapping

Quantitative analysis of how models and concepts move across scientific fields, using bibliographic data, citation networks, and text analysis.
